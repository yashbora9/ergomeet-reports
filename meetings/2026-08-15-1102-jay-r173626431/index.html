Purpose of the Meeting

Motivation of the team for the „Run to the Sun“ competition through an explanation of the business model.

Wichtigste Erkenntnisse

    • „Run to the Sun“-Wettbewerb: Win a free 3-day trip to Spain by recruiting 3 direct partners who each produce 99 units.
    • Strategische Rekrutierung: Use the upcoming “Grundseminar” and the “Kick-off” event to introduce prospects to the company culture and business model.
    • The “Cashflow-Quadrant” pitch: Position the network-marketing model as the easiest way to move from employee (time for money) to business owner (systems for money).
    • Pitch-Taktik: Secure an appointment by offering a “test phase” that defers the decision to a later date and minimizes risk for the prospect.

Themen

„Run to the Sun“-Wettbewerb

    • Ziel: Win a free 3-day trip to Spain for two people.
    • Voraussetzung: Recruit 3 direct partners who each produce at least 99 units.
    • Zeitrahmen: 6+ Wochen verbleiben.

Bevorstehende Rekrutierungs-Events

    • Grundseminar (Samstag):
        • Zweck: Introduce prospects to the company culture and leadership.
        • Umfang: 150–200 participants, internal football matches.
    • Kick-off-Event (Sonntag):
        • Zweck: Show prospects the size and potential of the company.
        • Umfang: 300 participants, €50,000 budget, catering, top speakers from Munich.

Why the business model works

    • 1. Riesiger Markt:
        • Market of untapped potential: 83 % of Germans have no financial products.
        • Displacement market: Replace poor products (e.g., an ETF policy that delivered -0.7 % p. a. over 15 years) with better ones.
    • 2. Supportive environment:
        • Netzwerk: Connect with like-minded, goal-oriented people.
        • Work-Life-Integration: Merge work and private life by working with friends and family.
    • 3. Personal development:
        • Leadership: Take early responsibility for the success of a team.
        • Skills: Develop routines, strategy and self-discipline.

Recruitment pitch: The „Cashflow-Quadrant“

    • Konzept: Explain the four work categories to position the structure-distribution model.
        • Angestellter (E): Tauscht Zeit gegen Geld.
        • Self-employed (S): Trades his skills for money.
        • Business owner (B): Nutzt Systeme, um Geld zu verdienen.
        • Investor (I): Nutzt Geld, um Geld zu verdienen.
    • Positionierung: The structure-distribution model is the simplest way to move from employee to business owner.
        • System: A team of self-employed partners.
        • Vorteil: No salary costs, as ERGO pays the partners directly.

Pitch tactic for the events

    • Ziel: Secure an appointment for a follow-up conversation, not close the deal.
    • Rahmen: Offer a „test phase“ to minimize the risk for the prospect.
    • Formulierung: „Listen to it, form your own opinion and then decide whether it is something for you.“
    • Dringlichkeit: Emphasize that this is the last chance to experience the „basic seminar“.
