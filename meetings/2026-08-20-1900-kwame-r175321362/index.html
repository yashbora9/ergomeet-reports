Purpose of the Meeting

A reality check on the possibilities with Ergo Pro and the required efforts.

Wichtigste Erkenntnisse

    • Erfolgsformel: Define your goals, ignore the doubters and work so hard that success becomes the logical consequence.
    • Ergo Pro vs. traditionelle Karriere: Ergo Pro offers the potential for an annual income of over 100,000 € within 4 years, a feat that is unattainable in most traditional careers.
    • The 5-Phase Entrepreneur Cycle: Success depends on overcoming the “dangerous phase” of informed pessimism – the point at which most people give up.
    • The 139-Day Plan: Use the 20 questions for self-reflection to identify your three biggest weaknesses and create a concrete plan for the final 139 days of fiscal year 2026.

Themen

The Ergo-Pro Path vs. Traditional Career Paths

    • Traditional paths such as apprenticeships and university degrees offer no guarantee of high income or professional freedom.
    • Ergo Pro offers a direct path to financial freedom and self-employment.
        • Beispiel: Kwame reached Stufe 4 after 4 years and earned his first clearly five-figure monthly salary (26,000 €) after 4.5 years.
    • Stufe-4-Potenzial: An annual income of 150,000 € to 250,000 € is possible by writing 9,000 units in 6 months with a team of 20–40 people.

The 5-Phase Entrepreneur Cycle

    • A framework by Alex Hormozi to describe the psychological journey of an entrepreneur.
    • 1. Uninformierter Optimismus: Initial enthusiasm that overlooks the challenges.
    • 2. Informierter Pessimismus: The “dangerous phase” in which reality (rejections, difficulties) leads to disappointment and the desire to give up.
    • 3. Valley of Despair: The low point at which most people give up.
    • 4. Informierter Optimismus: After overcoming the low point, skills and self-confidence continue to develop.
    • 5. Erfolg: The long-term reward for perseverance.

The 139-Day Self-Reflection Challenge

    • A 20-question framework to evaluate performance and create a plan for the final 139 days of fiscal year 2026.
    • Beispielfragen:
        • How many hours did I *actively* work last week versus spend on time-wasting activities?
        • When was the last time I did something uncomfortable for my business?
        • Which excuse do I use most often?
        • Am I ready to sacrifice my ego for success?
