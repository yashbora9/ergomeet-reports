Purpose of the Meeting

Basti provides insights into the mindset and habits that lead to success.

Wichtigste Erkenntnisse

    • The environment shapes the norms: Environment and habits determine what one considers possible. To develop further, one must surround oneself with people who advance one and introduce routines that reduce decision fatigue.
    • Ego gegen Erfolg: Letting go of the ego is crucial for continuous learning. Basti’s breakthrough came when he let go of the idea that he knew everything alone and actively sought feedback, which took him from Stufe 4 to Stufe 5.
    • Reframing beliefs: Negative beliefs are the biggest barrier. One must identify them, transform them into positive affirmations, and reinforce these daily to reshape reality.
    • Perspective is crucial: The view of setbacks determines the outcome. An attitude of gratitude and learning turns failures into opportunities and prevents giving up just before success.

Themen

The environment shapes the norms

    • The environment determines what one considers normal.
    • Beispiel: Basti’s friends adapted their language and behavior to the academics they surrounded themselves with.
    • To develop further, one must surround oneself with people who advance one.
    • Beispiel: Basti reduced contact with his mother to protect himself from her negativity.
    • Routines reduce decision fatigue and create mental capacity for more important tasks.
    • Beispiel: Steve Jobs and Mark Zuckerberg always wore the same clothes.

Ego gegen Erfolg

    • Letting go of the ego is the key to continuous learning.
    • Prinzip: If you are the smartest person in the room, you have finished the level and must move to a more challenging environment.
    • Basti’s breakthrough came when he let go of the idea that he knew everything alone and actively sought feedback.
    • Aktion: He completed 3–4 coaching sessions per week (Zoom/in-person) for 4–6 weeks.
    • Ergebnis: This approach enabled him to advance from Stufe 4 to Stufe 5.

Reframing beliefs

    • Negative beliefs are the biggest barrier to success.
    • Prinzip: If one can be negative without reason, one can also be positive without reason.
    • Methode:
      • Identifizieren: Einen negativen Glaubenssatz finden.
            • Beispiel: “I can’t build a team because I have nothing to show (e.g., a house).”
      • Umwandeln: In eine positive Affirmation umformulieren.
            • Beispiel: "I'm a leader; why shouldn't people follow me?"
      • Beweisen: Find evidence from the past that supports the new conviction.
            • Beispiel: Successful negotiations over a bank commission, even though one did not feel up to it.
      • Affirm: Repeat the new affirmation daily.
    • Zeitrahmen: It takes about 30 days to change a belief, and up to 60 days for deeply rooted convictions.

Perspective is crucial

    • The way one views setbacks determines the outcome.
    • Prinzip: You cannot be sad while you are grateful.
    • Beispiel: A customer who leaves is a setback, but also an opportunity to learn.
    • Analogie: Two reactions to spilled coffee:
        • Negative Reaktion: Anger and frustration.
        • Positive Reaktion: Gratitude that one reacted quickly enough to avoid an accident.
    • Ergebnis: A positive perspective prevents giving up just before success.

    • Prinzip: Man kann nicht traurig sein, solange man dankbar ist.
    • Beispiel: Ein Kunde, der abspringt, ist ein Rückschlag, aber auch eine Chance zum Lernen.
    • Analogie: Zwei Reaktionen auf einen verschütteten Kaffee:
        • Negative Reaktion: Wut und Frustration.
        • Positive Reaktion: Dankbarkeit, dass man schnell genug reagiert hat, um einen Unfall zu vermeiden.
    • Ergebnis: Eine positive Sichtweise verhindert, dass man kurz vor dem Erfolg aufgibt.
