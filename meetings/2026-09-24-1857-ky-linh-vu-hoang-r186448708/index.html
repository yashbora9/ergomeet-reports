Script

Question list

All 37 questions from this call. German is the spoken script. English is the meaning. Ask about the path, not the result. Stay on the season that gets the strongest response.

Color diagnosis (3)

Introvert = green + blue. Extrovert = yellow + red. Logical = blue + red. Emotional = green + yellow. Deliberate = blue + green. Fast = yellow + red. The color that hits twice is the tendency. Office and private life can differ.

  1. Bin ich eher introvertiert oder extrovertiert?
    Am I more introverted or extroverted?
  2. Bin ich eher logisch oder emotional?
    Am I more logical or emotional?
  3. Spreche ich bewusst (kalkuliert) oder schnell?
    Do I speak deliberately, or quickly?

Spring — Future (7)

  1. Worauf freust du dich?
    What are you looking forward to?
  2. Was würdest du gerne ausprobieren, hast dich bisher aber noch nicht getraut?
    What would you like to try, but have not dared to yet?
  3. Wenn du an deinen nächsten Lebensabschnitt denkst, was soll sich anders anfühlen?
    When you think about your next chapter, what should feel different?
  4. Was begeistert dich an deinem Vorhaben so sehr, dass du dafür auch Schwierigkeiten in Kauf nehmen würdest?

Summer — Reflection (7)

  1. Worüber denkst du in letzter Zeit oft nach?

  2. Was war dir früher unglaublich wichtig und spielt heute kaum noch eine Rolle?

  4. Was hast du nach dieser Erfahrung anders gesehen?

  5. Gab es ein Gespräch, das verändert hat, wie du über dich selbst denkst?
    Was there a conversation that changed how you think about yourself?
  6. Was hat sich damals wie ein Rückschlag angefühlt und dich rückblickend weitergebracht?
    What felt like a setback then, but moved you forward in hindsight?
  7. Was hat dich in dieser Zeit beschäftigt, das die Menschen um dich herum gar nicht mitbekommen haben?

OverviewEnhanced summaryTap to expand

Purpose of the Meeting

Training on communication types and questioning competencies for more effective interactions.

Wichtigste Erkenntnisse

    • Vier Kommunikationstypen: Use a color model (Red, Blue, Green, Yellow) to identify a person's primary motivators and adapt your communication style accordingly.
    • The „Four Seasons“ questions: Use a framework of future, reflection, experience, and ideas to conduct deep, personal conversations that build trust.
    • The „X+1“ formula: Build charisma by taking one additional, positive step in every situation to stand out from the standard.
    • The „Why“ behind the result: Ask questions about the process that led to a result to discover a person's story and motivation.

Themen

Vier Kommunikationstypen

    • Rot (Ergebnisorientiert):
        • Merkmale: Direkt, schnell, entscheidungsfreudig.
        • Motivatoren: Macht, Ergebnisse, Effizienz, Erfolg.
        • Trigger: Long explanations, small talk, indecision.
        • Kommunikation: Be confident, direct, and start with the result.
    • Blau (Faktenorientiert):
        • Merkmale: Analytical, precise, fact-oriented, structured.
        • Motivatoren: Logic, accuracy, quality, transparency.
        • Trigger: Exaggerations, emotional appeals, vague statements.
        • Kommunikation: Be prepared, use data, and offer options for review.
    • Green (harmony-oriented):
        • Merkmale: Loyal, geduldig, vermeidet Konflikte.
        • Motivatoren: Security, trust, stability, personal relationships.
        • Trigger: Konfrontation, Unsicherheit, Druck zu schnellen Entscheidungen.
        • Kommunikation: Build trust, listen actively, and approach changes gradually.
    • Gelb (Begeisterungsorientiert):
        • Merkmale: Offen, kommunikativ, enthusiastisch, menschenorientiert.
        • Motivatoren: Enthusiasm, recognition, freedom, fun, new opportunities.
        • Trigger: Dry details, numbers, long presentations.
        • Kommunikation: Build energy, use stories and memes, and focus on the feeling of the future.

The „Four Seasons“ questions

    • Spring (future):
        • „Worauf freust du dich?“
        • „What would you like to try that you haven't dared to do yet?“
    • Sommer (Reflexion):
        • „What was incredibly important to you in the past and hardly plays a role today?“
        • „What felt like a setback but, in retrospect, moved you forward?“
    • Herbst (Erfahrung):
        • „What went through your mind just before you agreed?“
        • „What would have almost made you decide differently?“
    • Winter (Ideen):
        • „How did you even come up with this idea?“
        • „What do you have a strong opinion about?“

Charisma through the „X+1“ formula

    • Konzept: Always take one positive step more than the standard to stand out.
    • Beispiele:
        • In the elevator: Give a compliment instead of just saying „Hello“.
        • On the train: Smile instead of a neutral expression.
        • At a meeting: Shake hands with everyone.
