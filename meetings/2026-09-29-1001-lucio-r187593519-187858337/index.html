Themen

The „Main Character“ mentality

    • Be the “Main Character” of your story, not an NPC.
    • Create an exciting story that attracts the attention of the universe.
    • Ignore the opinions of others; your truth is your own.
    • Proclaim your story with energy and frequency.

    • Hear: Listen to a motivational playlist to hear the sounds of your success.

The result-oriented cycle

    • Successful people start with the *result* and create a positive cycle:
        • Ergebnis: Visualize and experience your goal as already achieved.
        • Potenzial: The result unleashes your full potential.
        • Aktion: The released potential leads to Peak Performance.
        • Glaube: Peak Performance generates a strong, unshakable belief.
    • Vergleich: NPCs start with belief. Doubt → limited potential → poor actions → poor results → even more doubt.

Pay the price

    • Success requires an investment of time and effort.
    • Beispiel: The first 2–5 years of your business are an investment, not a loss.
        • 2 Jahre ohne Gewinn → 1.000 € Gewinn

Purpose of the Meeting

A 6-step plan to reach Stufe 3 in the next 99 days.

Wichtigste Erkenntnisse

    • Dringlichkeit: There are 99 days left until the end of the year (January 6) to reach Stufe 3 – a crucial milestone that transforms mindset and financial situation by 180°.
    • The “Why” is the engine: A deep, emotional “Why” is the engine for success. Use the “5-Whys” method to find your core motivation, and use a Blacklist/Whitelist to visualize it.
    • Hindernisse beseitigen: Success is subtraction, not addition. Remove distractions and negative influences. The “Taddeus-Corona exercise” evaluates your environment; a score below 2.0 indicates an environment that actively hinders success.
    • The tactic is simple: All recruitment methods work. The plan is to win 8–15 direct new entrants and supply them with 15 VG appointments. The challenge lies in consistent execution, not in the strategy.

Themen

The 99-day challenge to Stufe 3

    • Stufe 3 is the most important milestone in the business, requiring a complete personal and professional transformation.
    • The fight for Stufe 3 is a “turning point” that transforms mindset, self-confidence, and financial situation by 180°.
    • Lucio reached Stufe 3 after starting with 0 units in September 2024, proving that success is achievable for everyone.

Schritt 1: Ziel setzen

    • The goal is clear: reach Stufe 3 by January 6, 2027.

Step 2: Define the “Why”

    • A strong “Why” is the engine that drives execution.
    • „5-Whys“-Methode: Frage „Warum?“ mindestens 3–5 Mal, um deine Kernmotivation zu finden.
        • *Example:* “I want money.” → “Why?” → “For recognition.” → “Why?” → “To prove to myself that I can be successful.”
    • Visualisierungstools:
        • Blacklist: Personen, denen du deinen Erfolg beweisen willst.
        • Whitelist: People who have supported you and whom you want to thank.
        • Zielcollage: A visual representation of your life after reaching Stufe 3.

Schritt 3: Hindernisse beseitigen

    • Success is subtraction. Remove distractions before you begin execution.
    • Ablenkungsliste: List all things that distract you from focus.
    • Verzichtsliste: List all things you will give up for the next 99 days.
    • The “Taddeus-Corona exercise”:
      • List the 10 people with whom you spend the most time.
      • Rate each person: 3 (helps you achieve your goals), 2 (on equal footing), 1 (holds you back).
      • Calculate the average value.
            • Ergebnis \< 2,0: Your environment actively hinders success.
            • Ergebnis \> 2,2: Your environment supports success.

Schritt 4: Fokus aufbauen

    • Focus is the value of your word to yourself.
    • Fokus-Tools:
        • Morgenroutine: Start the day with clear focus.
        • „Stille Stunde“: One hour without a phone to reflect and plan.
        • 21 reasons for success: List 200–250 reasons for success daily to internalize the “why”.
        • Abendliche Reflexion: End the day with a review of progress and planning for the next day.

Step 5: Implement the plan

    • The strategy is simple: win 8–15 direct starters and supply them with 15 VG appointments.
    • Rekrutierung: All five methods work. The challenge lies in consistent implementation.
        • Lucio-Beispiel: 8 direct starters were won through a mix of name list, DK, social media, and networking.
    • Struktur: First win starters to build momentum. Only then build strategic “chains” once you have enough starters to place.

Schritt 6: Durchziehen

    • Stick consistently to the plan without deviations.
    • These 6 steps are a universal success recipe that can be applied to any goal.
