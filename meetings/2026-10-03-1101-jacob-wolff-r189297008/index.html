Purpose of the Meeting

Motivation and strategies for overcoming setbacks and achieving goals.

Wichtigste Erkenntnisse

    • Success is a decision. The transformation of David Goggins from 134 kg to a Navy SEAL and Jakobs own path from 110 kg to natural bodybuilder show that one can recreate their identity by choosing discipline and new challenges.
    • The mind is the greatest limit. The body can do more than the mind allows. The “40% rule” states that one has only reached 40% of their physical limit when the mind wants to give up; the real battle is mental.
    • Setbacks are opportunities for growth. Jakobs burnout after bodybuilding and his failure with a T-shirt business were the catalysts for his entry at Ergomeet, which forced him to realign his goals and build a new identity.
    • Record successes to maintain motivation. A “success journal” (like Rudolf Schiffer’s) serves as concrete proof of progress and provides the necessary mental strength in difficult times.

Themen

The power of transformation

    • David Goggins: A role model for extreme transformation.
        • Ausgangspunkt: At 24 years old, 134 kg, unemployed and without discipline.
        • Ziel: Become a Navy SEAL, despite the assessment of being “too fat and black”.
        • Ergebnis: Lost 45 kg in 3 months and completed “Hell Week” three times (once because his team failed) to prove his mental strength.
    • Jakobs Weg: A personal transformation that reflects Goggins’ principles.
        • Ausgangspunkt: At 15 years old, 110 kg, with an unhealthy environment in gastronomy (10–16-hour shifts, alcohol, smoking).
        • Wendepunkt: A single mentor believed in him and triggered a change.
        • Ergebnis: Lost 30 kg, became a natural bodybuilder and reached the European championship.

Overcoming the mental limit

    • The 40% rule: When the mind wants to give up, the body has only reached 40% of its true limit.
    • Goggins' Beispiel: He ran an ultra-marathon without ever having run a marathon before, proving that mental strength can overcome physical preparation.
    • Jakobs Burnout: After bodybuilding and a demanding security job (60 hours/week, 30 hours commute) he suffered burnout and was bedridden for 16 weeks.
        • Erkenntnis: Success is not sustainable if the mind does not participate.

Using setbacks as opportunities

    • Failure of the T-Shirt business: Jakob's first self-employment failed when his business partner embezzled the entire start-up capital.
    • Entry at Ergomeet: After the business failure and a breakup, he urgently needed money and ended up at Ergomeet, where he was interviewed by Leandro.
        • Leandros entscheidende Frage: „How will your life look if you continue down this path?“ forced Jakob to question his future.
    • Challenges at Ergomeet:
        • Schlechter Start: Showed up unprepared and inappropriately dressed on the first day, but was given a chance.
        • Business partner conflict: A business partner suggested selling cocaine to make quick money, leading to a separation.
        • Mental strength: Jakob used a 7-hour Goggins audio file to push through and eventually recruited a friend who thought he was a failure.

The Ergomeet opportunity

    • The reality of most jobs: Most people are stuck in dead-end jobs, fear getting sick, and strongly identify with companies that do not value them.
    • Ergomeet als Ausweg: Offers a chance for financial success and personal development that is rare in traditional jobs.
    • Independent thinking: Successful team members must act like entrepreneurs and take responsibility for their results instead of waiting for instructions.
